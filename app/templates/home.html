How it works

The AI looks. Fixed rules decide.

The model is never shown the order, so it can't simply agree with it. It lists what it sees; plain rules compare that list with the order, one check at a time. Every answer can be explained, and a person can always overrule it.

  1. 1

    Pick the order

    From the day's list, imported as a simple CSV: order ID and lines like SKU-A:2.

  2. 2

    Photograph the open box

    A guide on screen says what each shot should show. Blurry or dark photos are turned back at once, before any AI call.

  3. 3

    Follow the answer

    In a few seconds: what's wrong, and what to change. If the AI doesn't answer, the box waits for a person. It is never sealed by default.

Check by hand

The photo can't settle it, such as stacked items or a hidden label. A person looks before sealing.
