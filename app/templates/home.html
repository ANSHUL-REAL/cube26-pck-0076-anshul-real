Mis-ships are cheap to cause and expensive to fix.

A wrong item, a short count or an extra product means a refund, a reship and an unhappy buyer. Big warehouses buy camera stations for this. Pack Manager does it with the phone the packer already has, for sellers and 3PLs who pack their own orders.

And when a buyer says "the box was empty", the seller has a timestamped photo of what went in.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pick the order

    From the day's list, imported as a simple CSV: order ID and lines like SKU-A:2.

  2. 02

    Photograph the box

    A guide on screen says what each shot should show. Blurry or dark photos are turned back at once, before any AI call.

  3. 03

    Follow the answer

    In a few seconds: what's wrong and what to change. If the AI doesn't answer, the box waits for a person. Never sealed by default.

Three answers. "Not sure" is one of them.
