How to check this record

Download the record ({{ r.record_id }}.json) from its page, then run:

python -m pack_manager check-record {{ r.record_id }}.json --photo photo.jpg

It recomputes the content hash (SHA-256 of the record's canonical JSON) and each earlier version's hash, and says whether a photo is one of this record's. A matching hash means the file and photos are the ones the hash was computed from. It is not tamper-proof: whoever can change a record can recompute its hash. It doesn't prove when the record was made, or what was outside the photo.
