  • Go in order: D01 to D20 (practice), then T01 onwards (test).
  • Red lines are where the box is meant to differ from the order.
  • One photo from above with the whole inside of the box in the frame (a second from an angle is optional).
  • Wait about 30 seconds before the next box, so the photos can be grouped by time.
  • If you pack a box differently from the plan, tell Claude the box number before importing.
