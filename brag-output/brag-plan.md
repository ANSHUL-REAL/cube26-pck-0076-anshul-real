# /brag plan: Pack Manager

**What it is:** a web app that checks an open shipping box against its order from one phone photo, says Seal / Stop and fix / Check by hand, and keeps the photo and answer as the record of what was sent.
**For:** sellers and 3PLs who pack their own orders, with no camera station or scanner.
**What sets it apart:** the agent is never shown the order; it lists what it sees and fixed rules decide. "Not sure" is a first-class answer. Every box leaves a record that Returns and Recovery can read.
**Most impressive true claim:** on the organisers' sample, the rules stopped all 4 wrong boxes; the person in the data sealed 2 of them. Real-photo results are published, misses included.
**Visual hook:** a real open box (held-out test box T17) with an extra jar candle that the model circles in red.
**Real UI shown:** the front page's photo card (real model output), the phone "check a box" screen, a record page, the three answer chips.
**Tone:** `default`, punchy and playful, in the product's own identity (pink #f19be4, mustard #f7c948, cream #fdf6e6, ink #17131a, orange #ff7a1a, Inter Tight 800/900, black outlines with solid shadows).
**Share caption:** one photo of the open box, and Pack Manager says whether it's safe to seal.

**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 22 s. Music 120 BPM, scene cuts on the beat.
